# Roadmap

## Now

A public HBB study, coding substitutions, protein translation, a researcher and skeptic, and a local evidence notebook. An interactive landing page reads Python-generated reports.

## Next

Add a second well-characterized public gene and independent benchmark fixtures. Add reproducible computational comparisons with source files linked to observations. Evaluate hypothesis quality against held-out evidence before claiming improvement. Teach one biology concept per study.

## Later

Design a human-reviewed experiment record with controls, measurement units, outcomes, and provenance. Choose a specific instrument with a qualified lab partner before writing an adapter. Simulate commands, test interlocks, and require explicit human authorization before any physical action.

Real lab experiments and automation are future work. No autonomous experiments, model training, structure prediction, or instrument control ship in the first release.
